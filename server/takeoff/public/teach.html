Teach the take-off from your past jobs

Add a past job and drop in whatever you have for it: the quote or proposal, estimate sheets, material lists, supplier quotes, specifications, a job cost report with actual hours, even photos of handwritten sheets. The AI reads them and proposes what your shop does: scope it includes and excludes, finishes, outside costs, prices paid, tons and hours. You confirm each one; nothing is used until you do. Your files are kept, private to your shop, so they can be read again as the take-off improves.
